## Observations (not source quotations)

- A static merchant QR is an official no-code merchant acceptance product, created/configured through Merchant Portal for a branch/store/counter.
- It is materially different from a seller’s personal receive-money QR: merchant onboarding/configuration and merchant-side success confirmation are part of this product.
- Therefore an arbitrary seller-uploaded QR image must not be presented as a platform-issued merchant QR or as proof that the platform received a payment event.
