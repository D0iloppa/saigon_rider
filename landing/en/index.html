Frequently asked questions

Who can apply as a partner?

Shops, restaurants, motorbike repair shops and services operating in Ho Chi Minh City. All you need is your business name, address, category and contact details.

How long does the review take?

We review applications by hand, aiming to respond within 24 business hours. Applications that do not meet our standards may be declined with a specific reason.

How much does advertising cost?

Pricing is shared directly when you get in touch. During the launch period, email us for early-partner consultation.

Can I reapply if I was declined?

Yes. You can update your information based on the decline reason and submit again at any time.
